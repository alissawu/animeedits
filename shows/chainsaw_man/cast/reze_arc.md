# Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc (MAPPA, 2025)

## Cast
### Denji
- Hair: messy, spiky blond/yellow hair
- Eyes: amber/brown, rough look; sharp shark-like teeth when grinning
- Outfit: white dress shirt with loose black necktie and black trousers (Public Safety devil hunter uniform), which he also wears to the summer festival; casual t-shirts off duty
- Accessories: a pull cord (starter rope) sticking out of his chest
- Other forms: Chainsaw Man — head replaced by a chainsaw blade, chainsaw blades bursting out of both forearms, dark torso, often blood-soaked

### Reze
- Hair: short dark hair (black with purple/violet tint), chin-length bob, bangs
- Eyes: green
- Outfit: café apron at the Crossroads café; a dark top with a backpack on her first day; a white sleeveless blouse with a black ribbon tie and hair in a low bun at the summer festival; a white shirt and denim skirt with a cap at the end; nude in the school pool at night
- Accessories: a black choker around her neck with a ring/grenade pin on it (the pin she pulls to transform)
- Other forms: Bomb Devil — head replaced by a dark bomb-like head with a gas-mask/fuse shape and a long fuse on top, smoke and fire around her, explosions from her hands

### Makima
- Hair: bright crimson red, straight and neat: blunt bangs, long locks framing the face, the rest in one long braid down her back
- Eyes: golden yellow with concentric rings, like a target
- Outfit: white dress shirt, black necktie, black trousers; a long dark coat outdoors
- Never has a halo or wings. Her red is brighter and her hair sleeker than the Angel Devil's messy auburn.

### Aki Hayakawa
- Hair: black hair tied in a small topknot
- Eyes: dark blue, tired expression
- Outfit: black suit, white shirt, black tie
- Accessories: small earrings; carries a katana on his back

### Power
- Hair: long pale blonde/pinkish hair
- Eyes: orange/yellow with cross-shaped pupils
- Outfit: devil hunter uniform or a hoodie
- Accessories: two curved red horns on her head

### Beam
- Other forms: Shark Fiend — a shark head on a human body in a suit; also a large shark that Chainsaw Man rides

### Typhoon Devil
- A huge spinning mass of red-purple flesh with a small face on top and a giant leg

### Angel Devil
- Hair: auburn (orange-brown red), shaggy and uneven, chin to shoulder length, with one pointed lock hanging down between the eyes
- Eyes: magenta/pinkish red, droopy and half-lidded; a bored, sleepy look
- Outfit: the same Public Safety uniform as Makima and Aki: black suit jacket, white shirt, black tie
- Accessories: a thin yellow halo floating above the head and large white feathered wings on the back. A red-haired figure with a halo or wings is always the Angel Devil, never Makima.
- An androgynous young man, usually beside Aki, his partner

### Pochita
- A small orange dog-like devil with a chainsaw sticking out of its forehead

### Café owner (Master)
- Dark-haired man with a mustache and an apron behind the counter of the Crossroads café

### Kishibe
- Older man, short dark hair, scarred face, stubble, black suit
