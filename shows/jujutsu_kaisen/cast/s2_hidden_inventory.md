# Jujutsu Kaisen season 2, Hidden Inventory / Premature Death arc (MAPPA, 2023)

## Cast

Lighting: some scenes are strongly tinted (red, blue, amber or sepia), which shifts hair and clothing colors. Under tinted light, match on shape instead: hairstyle, outfit cut and accessories.

Setting: 2006–2007. Gojo, Geto and Shoko are teenage students at Jujutsu High. Nobody from the 2018 story (Yuji, Nobara, Kenjaku) appears in these episodes.

### Satoru Gojo
- Hair: snow-white, spiky and messy. As a student (2006–07) it falls loose over his forehead. As an adult the black blindfold pushes it straight up into spikes; with sunglasses it falls loose again
- Eyes: vivid glowing cyan-blue ("Six Eyes") with long white eyelashes. Usually hidden behind sunglasses (student) or a blindfold (adult)
- Outfit: as a student: Jujutsu High uniform, a dark navy high-collared jacket with one gold swirl button on the right chest and slim straight navy pants; tall and lean. Student alternates: untucked white button-up shirt with rolled sleeves (gym, ep 1); open red hooded jacket over a bare chest with dark swim trunks, or a red hoodie (Okinawa beach, ep 3); plain white T-shirt (2007, ep 5); all-black high-collared clothes (Shinjuku and with young Megumi, ep 5). As an adult (2018 / Shibuya): dark navy-black high-collared long jacket and black pants
- Accessories: student: small round sunglasses with thin silver wire frames and dark blue-black lenses, often pushed down his nose. Adult: wide black cloth blindfold over the eyes. Holds a clear umbrella in the ep 1–5 ending song
- Other forms: baby (ep 11 flashback): newborn with white hair and white eyelashes, swaddled in white, pale washed-out frame. Child (ep 3 flashback): white hair, blue eyes, light patterned kimono. Child (ep 11 flashback): blue-grey hoodie, black track pants with a white side stripe, extreme close-ups of a glowing blue iris. "Awakened" student (ep 4): no sunglasses, blood running down his forehead, hair hanging over his eyes, jacket open over a blood-soaked white shirt, manic grin, glowing eyes. Adult in the ep 11 opening: pulls the blindfold down to show one blue eye
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto (same student uniform, but black hair in a bun, baggy pants and earrings); Mei Mei and Uraume (also white or silver hair, but a long braid or ponytail, or a blunt chin-length bob with monk robes); Toge Inumaki (platinum hair, but his collar covers his mouth). Under red light (end of ep 4, ep 11 opening) his hair reads pink; it is still long and spiky, unlike Yuji's short pink undercut with its red hood. Student vs adult: sunglasses with the student uniform vs blindfold

### Suguru Geto
- Hair: black with a blue-green sheen. As a student it is pulled up into a small bun on the crown, with one long bang hanging down one side of his face. In 2007 (ep 5) he also wears it fully down and loose to the shoulders. After he defects (ep 5) it is longer, half-up in a bun with long loose strands behind
- Eyes: narrow, dark, heavy-lidded; in 2007 (ep 5) thinner face with dark under-eye shadows
- Outfit: as a student: the same navy high-collared uniform jacket with a gold swirl button as Gojo, but with very wide, baggy pants gathered at the ankle; broad-shouldered. Alternates: untucked white button-up shirt (gym, ep 1); open navy aloha shirt with big yellow flowers over a bare chest (Okinawa, ep 3); pale blue T-shirt with black baggy pants and sandals (talking with Yuki, ep 5); light blue collared shirt (the village, ep 5); plain black long-sleeve top and black baggy pants (Shinjuku, ep 5); Buddhist priest robes, a dark kimono with a green-and-gold patchwork kasaya, holding a microphone on a stage (taking over the cult, ep 5)
- Accessories: large dark navy gauge earrings in stretched earlobes (visible in almost every close-up)
- Other forms: summons cursed spirits from black portals (see the curse entries). Wet hair hanging over his face in the shower (ep 5)
- Tell apart from: Kenjaku (same face and priest robes, but a stitched scar across the forehead and long flowing hair; Shibuya only); Satoru Gojo (white hair); Toji Fushiguro (black hair falling flat, no bun or earrings, scar at the mouth); Choso (two short pigtails, blood stripe across the nose)

### Shoko Ieiri
- Hair: brown. As a student, a short chin-length bob with bangs swept to one side. As an adult, long, straight and past the shoulders
- Eyes: brown, slightly sleepy, with a small mole under one eye; as an adult, dark tired lines under the eyes
- Outfit: as a student: navy Jujutsu High jacket with a matching short pencil skirt, grey-brown tights and brown loafers. As an adult: white lab coat over a blue-teal turtleneck, navy cropped pants and beige heels
- Accessories: cigarette, or a lollipop stick in her mouth
- Other forms: the adult appears only briefly in the ep 11 opening song
- Tell apart from: Nobara Kugisaki (also a brown bob, but orange-brown and with a tool belt and hammer; 2018 setting with Yuji and Megumi); Mimiko Hasaba (brown bob but a sailor uniform); Utahime Iori (black hair, shrine-maiden clothes)

### Utahime Iori
- Hair: long blackish-purple hair with blunt straight-cut bangs; two front locks hang over the shoulders, each bound with red-and-black ties
- Eyes: brown, often scowling or comically angry
- Outfit: shrine-maiden (miko) outfit: white wide-sleeved top, red pleated hakama with a red bow, brown boots
- Accessories: none notable
- Other forms: 2006 (eps 1 and 3, opening song) has no scar. Adult (not in these episodes): a scar across the nose and right cheek
- Tell apart from: Riko Amanai (similar dark hair, but a sailor uniform and white headband); Misato Kuroi (black maid dress); Mei Mei (silver hair, black suit)

### Mei Mei
- Hair: silver-white with a blue tint. In 2006, a high ponytail with two long front locks framing the face. As an adult (Shibuya), one thick braid hanging down over the front of her face, covering one eye
- Eyes: usually closed or narrowed in a smile (purple in the anime)
- Outfit: 2006 (ep 1): black suit jacket and black shirt with a grey tie, long dark skirt, black lace-up heeled boots. Adult: dark purple long-sleeved dress with puffed shoulders, baggy purple pants tucked into light brown boots
- Accessories: 2006: a closed black umbrella used as a cane. Adult: a huge battle axe and crows; often with her little brother Ui Ui
- Other forms: the adult appears only briefly in the ep 11 opening song
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (short spiky white hair, male); Uraume (white chin-length bob, monk robes)

### Kento Nanami
- Hair: blond with a neat side part
- Eyes: hazel, narrow and serious; as an adult hidden behind goggles
- Outfit: as a student (2006–07): standard navy Jujutsu High uniform. As an adult (Shibuya): beige-tan suit, blue shirt, olive leopard-spotted tie
- Accessories: student: carries a long dark bag over his shoulder. Adult: green-tinted, armless goggle-style sunglasses; a blade wrapped in spotted cloth
- Other forms: student sitting in the morgue with an arm over his face (ep 5); shows up in the student group of the ep 1–5 ending song. In episode 11 only as white line-art in the next-episode preview and a brief shot in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Yu Haibara (dark brown bowl cut); Yuki Tsukumo (long blonde hair, woman); Akari Nitta (blonde bob, black suit, woman); Takuma Ino (black beanie)

### Masamichi Yaga
- Hair: dark, buzzed short with two shaved stripes running along the sides of the head (2006–07); spikier on top as an adult
- Eyes: small and stern under thick eyebrows; mustache plus goatee; tan skin
- Outfit: black zip-up track jacket with a pale zipper line and black pants; very large, muscular build
- Accessories: no sunglasses in 2006–07; tinted sunglasses as an adult
- Other forms: the adult appears only briefly in the ep 11 opening song
- Tell apart from: Shiu Kong (suit, no goatee); Jiro Awasaka (bald with a topknot tuft, handlebar mustache); Shigeru Sonoda (white tunic, bump on the forehead)

### Yu Haibara
- Hair: short dark brown with straight bowl-cut bangs
- Eyes: big, round, bright, eager; wide grin
- Outfit: Jujutsu High jacket cropped short and worn open over a white T-shirt, navy pants, brown shoes
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: dead on a morgue table under a white sheet with blood, head visible (ep 5)
- Tell apart from: Megumi Fushiguro (spiky black hair); Kento Nanami (blond); teen Geto (bun)

### Riko Amanai
- Hair: very dark navy-black hair with bangs (a strand between the eyes), the rest in one long braid down the back
- Eyes: big, bright blue
- Outfit: school uniform: white long-sleeved sailor blouse with a navy white-striped collar and pale blue scarf, navy pleated skirt, black tights, brown loafers. Alternates: teal bikini with an open white hoodie and a yellow straw hat with a red flower (Okinawa beach); light blue dress with ruffled sleeves (aquarium); white frilly blouse (Okinawa lunch)
- Accessories: a wide cream-white cloth headband, almost always on
- Other forms: small child in a black dress with white socks (ep 3 memory); her body after being shot in the head, lying on a table or carried by Gojo wrapped in a white sheet (eps 4–5)
- Tell apart from: her classmates (same sailor uniform, but no headband and no braid); Utahime Iori (miko clothes); Misato Kuroi (maid)

### Misato Kuroi
- Hair: dark brown-black, pulled into a bun under a white bun cover, two long bangs framing the face
- Eyes: dark, calm, thin eyebrows
- Outfit: maid-style uniform: black long-sleeved dress with a white collar, white apron, dark grey tights, black shoes. Okinawa: black-and-white rash-guard swimsuit with the hair still in a bun
- Accessories: fights with a mop
- Other forms: kidnapped, tied up and gagged in the maid dress (ep 3); younger version in a navy maid dress (Riko's memory, ep 3)
- Tell apart from: Riko (sailor uniform, headband); Shoko (navy uniform, bob)

### Toji Fushiguro
- Hair: straight black, messy, falling over the forehead and to the ears
- Eyes: narrow, dark, lazy and smirking (green per the wiki); in ep 11 night scenes they glow pale blue
- Outfit: very muscular build. Casual (betting at the races, café): baggy dark grey or black long-sleeved sweatshirt and dark pants. Fighting: tight black short-sleeved shirt, very wide white or light-grey baggy pants with a dark sash, black slip-on sandals
- Accessories: a scar at the right corner of the mouth (key); a fat purple worm-like curse wrapped around his shoulders like a scarf; weapons: katana, the Inverted Spear of Heaven (short dagger with a forked guard), a long chain, a handgun
- Other forms: young, in the Zenin clan (ep 3 flashback): longer hair, grey-blue haori over a dark kimono, snowy estate. Dying (ep 4): half of his body blown away. Summoned in ep 11: Toji's face and black hair on Ogami's grandson's body, wearing a loose light-blue or white knit sweater with a ribbed collar, black pants and dark sneakers; a glowing, cracked face mid-transformation
- Tell apart from: Megumi Fushiguro (hair spikes outward, school uniform, no scar); Suguru Geto (bun and earrings); Shiu Kong (suit); Ogami's grandson before the séance (olive-green mohawk, his own face)

### Shiu Kong
- Hair: short black hair swept back, spiky on top
- Eyes: heavy-lidded, bored; stubble and a thin mustache
- Outfit: dark brown or black business suit, white shirt, dark green tie, brown shoes
- Accessories: cigarette
- Other forms: none
- Tell apart from: Toji (never in a suit); Kiyotaka Ijichi (glasses, thin); Shigeru Sonoda (white tunic)

### Shigeru Sonoda
- Hair: dark, slicked straight back
- Eyes: squinting, deeply wrinkled face with a round bump in the middle of the forehead
- Outfit: white high-collared zip-front tunic and pants (the cult's uniform)
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: dies on a stage in front of Geto (ep 5)
- Tell apart from: the Time Vessel Association crowd (same white tunics, no forehead bump); Masamichi Yaga

### Time Vessel Association members (Star Religious Group / "Children of the Star")
- Hair: assorted ordinary men and women, many middle-aged or elderly
- Eyes: smiling, eyes closed in bliss
- Outfit: identical white high-collared zip-front tunics and white pants
- Accessories: none; the group's emblem is a gold splash-like pattern on a black or purple panel
- Other forms: a packed crowd clapping in a pale blue-white hall around Gojo carrying Riko's body (end of ep 4), and again in Geto's head (ep 5)
- Tell apart from: Riko's classmates (sailor uniforms); Shigeru Sonoda (their leader, same tunic, forehead bump)

### Kokun
- Hair: buzz cut, mostly hidden under a cap
- Eyes: dark, triangular eyebrows
- Outfit: Q's military-style uniform: white peaked cap with a dark bill and a "Q" badge, white double-breasted coat, black cape lined in red with red shoulder tabs, dark shirt, green tie, white gloves, white pants and boots
- Accessories: black cloth mask over the nose and mouth
- Other forms: held down by one of Geto's curses (start of ep 2)
- Tell apart from: Bayer (same uniform, long black hair); Takuma Ino (full black balaclava, all-black clothes)

### Bayer
- Hair: long straight black hair hanging past the shoulders from under the cap
- Eyes: wide, light-colored
- Outfit: the same white Q uniform as Kokun, with dark pants and boots
- Accessories: black mask over nose and mouth; throws knives
- Other forms: beaten, sitting on the ground in a phone photo with Gojo flashing a peace sign (ep 2)
- Tell apart from: Kokun (buzz cut); Kuchisake-Onna (long black hair, but a trench coat and a monster mouth)

### Elderly Curse User (shikigami user at Riko's school)
- Hair: hidden under a white cloth head wrap
- Eyes: behind round wire-rimmed glasses; very wrinkled, grinning face
- Outfit: loose olive-khaki work clothes, sandals
- Accessories: throws a paper charm that becomes grey-black blob shikigami with big pink lips, rows of teeth and a purple tongue
- Other forms: flashback in black clothes, hugging a Shiba dog; ends with a bloody, broken face (ep 2)
- Tell apart from: Masamichi Yaga (black track jacket); Jiro Awasaka (bald with a topknot, mustache, no glasses)

### Bag-Mask Curse User
- Hair: hidden
- Eyes: a brown paper bag over the head, with an X drawn over one eye, a cut-out hole for the other, and a stitched-mouth drawing
- Outfit: white T-shirt over a very muscular build, olive cargo pants
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: up to five identical clones on rooftops (ep 2)
- Tell apart from: anyone else; the paper bag is unique

### Renchoku Girls' Academy students (Riko's classmates)
- Hair: assorted brown and black styles, no headbands
- Eyes: ordinary; often drawn as squashed chibi faces in comedy beats
- Outfit: the same white sailor blouse, navy collar, pale blue scarf and navy skirt as Riko; their music teacher wears a white blouse, with a long dark ponytail
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: a row of schoolgirls in the ep 1–5 ending song
- Tell apart from: Riko Amanai (white headband, long braid, blue eyes)

### Yuki Tsukumo
- Hair: very long, straight, pale blonde, down to the waist, two tufts framing the face
- Eyes: brown, confident
- Outfit: tall and athletic; black sleeveless turtleneck, blue jeans with a black belt, black boots
- Accessories: leather jacket slung over a shoulder; a dark dome helmet with aviator goggles on her motorbike
- Other forms: none
- Tell apart from: Kento Nanami (short blond hair, man); Akari Nitta (short blonde bob, suit)

### Mimiko Hasaba
- Hair: as a child, a short darker-brown bob with straight bangs. As a teen (2017–18), mid-length brown hair with straight bangs
- Eyes: brown, wary
- Outfit: as a child (ep 5): bruised and dirty in a cage with her twin, wearing a sleeveless dress; afterward a dark teal-black hoodie. As a teen: navy sailor uniform with a white scarf, grey knee socks, brown loafers
- Accessories: as a teen, a pink stitched doll with a rope noose around its neck
- Other forms: the teen version appears in Shibuya only (not in ep 11)
- Tell apart from: Nanako Hasaba (lighter caramel hair, in a bun as a teen, beige cardigan); Shoko Ieiri as a student (brown bob, but a navy jacket and skirt)

### Nanako Hasaba
- Hair: as a child, short light caramel hair. As a teen, caramel hair in a top bun with loose strands
- Eyes: brown
- Outfit: as a child (ep 5): sleeveless light dress, bruised, in a cage; afterward a cream-white hoodie. As a teen: beige cardigan over a sailor top with a navy collar and blue scarf, short navy skirt, loose white socks, brown loafers
- Accessories: as a teen, a green phone
- Other forms: the teen version appears in Shibuya only (not in ep 11)
- Tell apart from: Mimiko (darker hair, bob, doll)

### Megumi Fushiguro
- Hair: black, spiky, sticking out in every direction like a sea urchin (as a child and as a teen)
- Eyes: dark blue-grey, sharp and upturned (green in the manga)
- Outfit: as a teen (2018): dark navy high-collared Jujutsu High jacket with gold buttons, matching cropped pants, black socks, brown shoes; slim. As a child (ep 5, first grade): light blue T-shirt, black shorts, black backpack
- Accessories: makes hand signs (shadow animals) to summon shikigami: a black wolf, a big brown owl-bird, a green toad, a pink elephant, white rabbits
- Other forms: none
- Tell apart from: Toji Fushiguro (hair falls flat over the forehead, scar at the mouth, adult build); Yuji Itadori (pink hair); Yu Haibara (bowl cut); Satoru Gojo (white hair)

### Tsumiki Fushiguro
- Hair: brown
- Eyes: brown
- Outfit: young girl, seen tiny and far away on an apartment balcony, waving (ep 5)
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: none in these episodes
- Tell apart from: Mimiko and Nanako as children (they are in a cage or in hoodies)

### Rainbow Dragon
- Hair: none; long pale white scaly serpentine body
- Eyes: bulging glowing yellow eyes with spiral pupils
- Outfit: none
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: rams Toji through buildings, then gets split down the middle (ep 4)

### Kuchisake-Onna
- Hair: long, black and disheveled, with many eyes opening inside the hair
- Eyes: hidden; a face wrapped in bandages with a huge mouth of razor teeth
- Outfit: tall woman in a beige trench coat cinched at the waist, polka-dot legs
- Accessories: giant scissor blades
- Other forms: appears beside Rainbow Dragon (end of ep 3, ep 4)
- Tell apart from: Bayer (long black hair, but white uniform)

### Geto's giant worm curse
- Hair: none; enormous pale bone-white segmented grub or maggot with holes along its body
- Eyes: none visible; its open mouth is a green tunnel lined with rows of fangs
- Outfit: none
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: fills a school hallway and swallows the elderly curse user (ep 2); swallows Toji whole and gets sliced apart from inside in a burst of purple (ep 3)
- Tell apart from: Toji's inventory curse (purple, wrapped around Toji, has a face)

### Geto's other curses
- Hair: none
- Eyes: varied
- Outfit: none. A big pink manta-ray-shaped flying curse with round eyes that carries Riko and Kuroi (ep 1). An orange-brown skull-like face with hollow eyes and a gaping mouth, ringed by a green spiky collar, that swallows another curse (ep 1). Two squat grinning creatures with red ribbed caps and an eye on top, rising from black floor holes (ep 2). A grey upright oval with two stacked yellow eyes and a long grin that Riko pets (ep 3)
- Accessories: they emerge from black portals or swirls around Geto; he absorbs curses as small black balls
- Other forms: none
- Tell apart from: Toji's fly heads (small pink and brown bugs); the ep 2 elderly curse user's grey big-lipped shikigami (his, not Geto's)

### Toji's inventory curse
- Hair: none; fat, segmented purple-maroon worm or caterpillar
- Eyes: a wrinkled pug-like face with squinting eyes and buck teeth
- Outfit: none
- Accessories: stores and spits out weapons; Geto absorbs it after Toji dies (ep 4)
- Other forms: none
- Tell apart from: Geto's giant worm curse (pale, huge, no face)

### Toji's fly heads
- Hair: none; a swarm of small flying pink, purple and brown curses with wings, like bugs and pigs
- Eyes: tiny
- Outfit: none
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: fills the sky around Gojo (ep 3)
- Tell apart from: Megumi's rabbits (white, on the ground)
