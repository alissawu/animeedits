# Jujutsu Kaisen season 2, Shibuya Incident arc (MAPPA, 2023)

## Cast

Lighting: from the end of ep 7 almost everything is night: underground in Shibuya Station under washed-out blue-white, teal or greenish fluorescent light that makes skin pale and turns white hair and white clothes pale blue and pink hair beige; dark streets lit orange by fire and red by Jogo's lava (eps 15–17); a cyan ice field in the crater (eps 22–23). Strong tints shift colors everywhere: under red light pink, white and silver hair all read pink (Yuji, Sukuna, Gojo, Mahito, Uraume), and under cyan or green light pink and white hair read white or mint (Yuji and Sukuna can look white-haired like Gojo; Mei Mei's silver hair reads mint like Ui Ui's). Domains recolor the frame: Mahito's violet space of giant grasping hands (eps 7, 21), the Smallpox Deity's dark cyan graveyard (ep 14), Dagon's bright tropical beach (eps 14–15), Sukuna's crimson sky and his deep-red innate domain (eps 17, 21). Daylight scenes: Tokyo streets and a family restaurant (ep 6), Kyoto Jujutsu High at sunset (eps 6–7), a dam lake in rain (eps 6–7). Flashbacks and imagined scenes: soft pink-lavender (Yuko's middle school, ep 6), grey or sepia memories and overexposed pastel playground flashbacks (ep 8), warm hazy daylight and grey winter (Nobara's childhood village, ep 19), white voids (Jogo's fantasy, ep 16; Nobara's chairs, ep 19), white-blue snow (Mahito's forest, ep 21), lilac sparkles (Todo's imagination, ep 21). The opening song is drenched in red and mostly silhouettes; the ending song is sunny daytime Shibuya with a faded film-camera look; previews are white line drawings on black or grey; some episodes run credits over grey stills or the last fight instead. Under tinted light, match on shape instead of color: hairstyle, outfit cut, scars and accessories.

Setting: 2018, Tokyo. Episode numbers are season 2's (ep 6 to ep 23 are episodes 30 to 47 overall). Gojo, Shoko, Nanami, Mei Mei, Utahime, Yaga, Kusakabe and Naobito are adults; Yuji, Megumi, Nobara and the Kyoto students are teenagers; a man who looks like adult Geto is Kenjaku. Ep 6, daytime before Halloween: Yuji, Megumi and Nobara with Yuko Ozawa (a movie trailer on Yuji's phone shows unnamed actors and a worm-headed man), Todo and Mei Mei at ping-pong, Gakuganji's office, Utahime and the trio at Kokichi Muta's empty basement, Kokichi with Kenjaku and Mahito, his giant robot rising from a lake. Ep 7: Mahito vs the robot at a dam; Miwa at Kyoto Jujutsu High; then Halloween night, October 31: costumed crowds at the scramble crossing, the sorcerer teams briefed outside the curtain, Gojo arriving. Ep 8: Gojo on B5F facing Jogo, Hanami and Choso, with pastel flashbacks of Kenjaku and the curses in a playground; an unnamed assistant manager (long brown hair, black suit, red tie) briefs Mei Mei, Ui Ui and Yuji, and Yuji fights the grasshopper curse Ko-Guy in Meiji-Jingumae Station. Ep 9: Gojo fights Jogo, Hanami, Choso and Mahito on the B5F subway platform among crowds of civilians, and Kenjaku seals him; the cold open shows Yuji, Megumi, Nobara, Maki, Panda, Toge, Nanami and Todo; teenage Gojo and Geto, Riko Amanai, Misato Kuroi and the Time Vessel Association appear only in Gojo's memories, and Yuta and Rika only while Kenjaku talks about them. Ep 10: Mei Mei, Ui Ui, Mechamaru's communicator and Niji Ebina in a subway tunnel; the three sorcerer teams (Naobito, Maki and Nobara; Kusakabe and Panda; Nanami, Megumi and Ino); Ijichi, Haruta Shigemo and Uraume; the Hasaba twins with Kenjaku; Kasumi Miwa and Sukuna only in one imagined shot each; Ogami, her grandson and Awasaka only after the ending song. Ep 11: Yuji, Megumi and Ino fight Awasaka, Ogami and her grandson at Shibuya Central Tower; Toji appears only as the grandson transformed by Ogami's séance; flashbacks show Ino eating with Nanami in a warm-lit restaurant, Gojo as a baby and a child, and Ogami and Awasaka when younger, with the people they killed (unnamed civilians, such as a businessman in a white shirt in an apartment); Kenjaku, the curses, Mei Mei, Uraume and the Hasaba twins are not on screen in ep 11 except in the opening song. Ep 12: Nanami finds Ijichi; Mei Mei finishes Ebina; Toji kills Ogami; Nobara and Akari Nitta fight Haruta Shigemo until Nanami arrives; Kenjaku sets the Smallpox Deity on Mei Mei; Yuji meets Choso. Ep 13: Yuji vs Choso in a station corridor and a flooded bathroom; Toge in the street. Ep 14: the Smallpox Deity's domain; Nanami, Naobito and Maki vs Dagon and Dagon's beach domain; Megumi and Toji arrive. Ep 15: Toji kills Dagon; Jogo burns Nanami, Maki and Naobito; the Hasaba twins feed Yuji fingers and Sukuna kills them. Ep 16: Sukuna vs Jogo across the city; Megumi vs Toji; Kusakabe and Panda vs Manami Suda and Toshihisa Negi (with three hooded curse users whose faces are never shown, who are not Negi); Shoko and Yaga at an expressway toll gate with Yaga's cursed corpses (blob-, toad- and slug-like creatures, not Toad); Uraume greets Sukuna. Ep 17: Megumi summons Mahoraga; Sukuna vs Mahoraga and Malevolent Shrine; Yuji wakes among the ruins. Ep 18: burned Nanami dies to Mahito; Yuji vs Mahito; Nobara vs Mahito's double. Ep 19: Nobara's childhood village (Fumi, Fumi's father, Saori, village children and adults, all unnamed except Fumi and Saori); Mahito's touch on Nobara's face. Ep 20: Todo and Arata Nitta arrive; Kokichi's last message to Miwa on a train with the other Kyoto students. Ep 21: Yuji and Todo vs Mahito in streets covered by a carpet of flesh and in a flooded crater. Ep 22: Mei Mei and Ui Ui in a Kuala Lumpur hotel; Kenjaku absorbs Mahito; the Kyoto students, Kusakabe, Panda and Choso attack him; Uraume freezes them; Yuki Tsukumo arrives. Ep 23: Yuki and Kenjaku in the iced-over crater; Tsumiki in hospital; an aftermath montage of plain realistic officials, civilians and news (none of them sorcerers); a girl saved by Yuta in a convenience store; Yuta before the higher-ups (never drawn, only glowing shoji screens); Yuji on a bridge at dawn. Characters seen only in memories, imagined shots or one-shot inserts: Yu Haibara (eps 12, 18), Junpei Yoshino (eps 18, 23), Kechizu and Eso (eps 13, 22), Larue (ep 16), Nobuko Takada (eps 20–21), Kenjaku's Meiji-era body (ep 22), Choso's mother (ep 22, dark bun, yellow kimono), Miguel (ep 23). Hanami dies in ep 9, Ogami in ep 12, Dagon in ep 15, the Hasaba twins in ep 15, Jogo and Toji in ep 16, Shigemo in ep 17, Nanami in ep 18; Mahito is absorbed in ep 22. The opening song (all but ep 17) shows most of the cast, including Sukuna, Shoko, Yaga, Nitta, Gakuganji and the giant robot; the ending song shows Yuji, Megumi and Nobara in daytime Shibuya. The crowds of civilians, many in Halloween costumes (clowns, the Joker, angels, nuns, maids, witches, nurses, cat or bunny ears, sailor uniforms), and the paramedics, managers and passers-by are unnamed and are not any of the characters below.

### Satoru Gojo
- Hair: snow-white, spiky and messy. With the black blindfold on (eps 7–8, the opening song, memories) it is pushed straight up into spikes; with it off (from the end of ep 8 into ep 10) it falls loose over his forehead and eyes in messy spikes. As a student (memories, ep 9), and as a baby or child (ep 11 flashbacks), the same white hair falling loose
- Eyes: vivid glowing cyan-blue ("Six Eyes") with long white eyelashes; uncovered from the end of ep 8 through ep 10 and often in extreme close-up. Otherwise hidden behind the blindfold, or behind small round dark sunglasses (as a student, and in some memories as an adult)
- Outfit: as an adult: dark navy-black long jacket with a high collar zipped up to the chin, black pants; a black high-necked top with round sunglasses in Megumi's memory of a traditional house (ep 17). As a student (memories, ep 9): navy Jujutsu High uniform with a high collar and one gold swirl button, slim straight pants; tall and lean
- Accessories: adult: wide black cloth blindfold; blood smeared under his left eye and down his cheek from midway through ep 9 into ep 10 (also in the opening song). Student: small round sunglasses with thin silver wire frames and dark lenses
- Other forms: arrives at Shibuya out of a dark ripple (ep 7) and walks on air above the Hikarie crowd (ep 8). Bound by the Prison Realm (end of ep 9, start of ep 10): dark red fleshy tendrils with eyeballs wrap around him, then shrink into a small cube covered in eyes; inside it (ep 10), alone in darkness. In ep 23 the Prison Realm is a palm-sized dark grey stone cube with his cyan eyes set into its faces. Fights in a blur of cyan light streaks; Unlimited Void flashes as a starry cosmic void (ep 9). Memories and imagined shots: beside Utahime in a hazy grey memory (ep 6); in dark round sunglasses beside Yuji on a lava field in a sepia insert (ep 7); laughing on black (ep 8); imagined in daylight (ep 14) or beside Kenjaku in a tunnel (ep 12); on a veranda in Megumi's memory, pulling his sunglasses down (ep 17); the coffee-shirt flashback in a warm classroom (ep 20); sofas in Nobara's imagined white void (ep 19). As a student, the bloodied boy carrying Riko's body through a clapping crowd (ep 9). Baby (ep 11 flashback): newborn with white hair, swaddled in white. Child (ep 11 flashback): blue-grey hoodie, black track pants. Chibi doodles. In the opening song he pulls the blindfold down to show one blue eye
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto (same student uniform, but black hair in a bun, baggy pants and earrings); Mahito (long, straight grey-blue hair hanging past the shoulders, sometimes tied up in spiky bunches, stitched seams across the face and neck, one blue and one grey eye that don't glow); Mei Mei and Uraume (also white or silver hair, but a long braid, or a blunt chin-length bob with monk robes); Naobito Zenin (white slicked-back hair on an old man with a mustache); Toge Inumaki (platinum hair, but his collar covers his mouth); Ui Ui (teal hair, small boy in suspenders); Toshihisa Negi (a white bandage over one eye, not a black blindfold). Under red light his hair reads pink; it is still long and spiky, unlike Yuji's short pink undercut with its red hood. Under moonlight, cyan or green light Yuji and Sukuna's pink hair can read white: Sukuna has black face tattoos and the red hood, Yuji the red hood and, late in the arc, a bloody gash across the nose. Gojo never wears a red hood or a red scarf: the figure seen from behind in a long navy coat with a red hood at the neck in the sunny ending song is Yuji. Megumi holding a camera to his face in the ending song is not a blindfold

### Suguru Geto
- Hair: black with a blue-green sheen; as a student, short, pulled up into a small bun on the crown, with one long bang hanging down one side of his face
- Eyes: narrow, dark, heavy-lidded, often smiling
- Outfit: as a student: the same navy high-collared uniform jacket with a gold swirl button as Gojo, but with very wide, baggy pants gathered at the ankle; broad-shouldered
- Accessories: large dark navy gauge earrings in stretched earlobes (visible in almost every close-up)
- Other forms: appears only as a teenager in Gojo's memories (ep 9): the student with a bun and earrings leaning in beside young Gojo in a classroom, laughing with him, standing alone under trees. In 2018 the man with his face is Kenjaku
- Tell apart from: Kenjaku (same face, but long loose hair, priest robes and a stitched scar across the forehead; 2018); Satoru Gojo (white hair, sunglasses); Toji Fushiguro (black hair falling flat, no bun or earrings, scar at the mouth); Choso (two short tufts, blood stripe across the nose); Yuta Okkotsu (short messy black hair, white uniform)

### Kenjaku (in Suguru Geto's body)
- Hair: long, straight black hair flowing past the shoulders, the upper part tied in a bun on the crown, one long bang down the left side of the face; reads dark olive in overexposed daylight, violet under purple light
- Eyes: narrow, golden-brown, smug half-lidded and amused look
- Outfit: Buddhist priest robes: dark purple-black kimono and hakama with wide sleeves and a white under-collar, a dark green patchwork kasaya with gold borders and a gold strap over the left shoulder, white tabi with maroon or black cross-straps up the ankle and zori sandals. In the mahjong flashback (ep 9): a beige zip-up jacket with a high collar
- Accessories: a thick horizontal line of surgical stitches across the forehead (the key identifier); large dark gauge earrings. Holds the Prison Realm (eps 9–10, 22–23); blows soap bubbles from a small pink bottle (ep 8)
- Other forms: with Mahito and Kokichi in a hideout and on a dam watching the giant robot, and in a forest by day (eps 6–7); bright pastel playground flashbacks explaining the plan to the curses (ep 8); lifts off the top of his skull to show a brain with teeth (ep 9); his own right hand grabs his throat and chokes him (ep 10); in a subway tunnel with a long dark dragon-like serpent curse (eps 12, 14); standing over Mahito in a snowy imagined forest (ep 21); absorbing Mahito in pale ribbons and purple light, riding a flat manta-ray curse, one eye glowing white in purple light (ep 22); crouching with a palm on the ground to cast a huge lilac-white glyph (ep 23); summoning many curses (see Kenjaku's summoned curses). In an earlier body (ep 22, a brief sepia memory of Choso's): the Meiji-era Noritoshi Kamo, a middle-aged man with short black hair swept back, a deeply lined face, a thick black downturned mustache, the same forehead stitches and a dark kimono, under a large dark red-brown oiled-paper umbrella
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto as a student (short hair in a bun, navy uniform, no stitches; ep 9 memories only); Noritoshi Kamo (the present-day Kyoto student: long black hair too, but two front locks wrapped in white, a navy uniform, a bow, no stitches); Choso (spiky hair with two tufts, black bar across the nose, purple scarf); Aoi Todo (top-knot bun, but a scarred face and a huge build); Mimiko Hasaba (long dark hair, but a schoolgirl with bangs); Tsumiki Fushiguro (a red glyph on her forehead, not stitches)

### Shoko Ieiri

### Utahime Iori

- Eyes: brown, often scowling or comically angry
- Outfit: shrine-maiden (miko) outfit: white wide-sleeved top, red pleated hakama with a red bow, brown boots
- Accessories: a broad pale scar across the nose and both cheeks
- Other forms: leads Yuji, Megumi and Nobara through a parking garage and a basement to Kokichi Muta's empty decoy room (ep 6); sitting beside Gojo in a hazy grey memory (ep 6); with the Kyoto students on a train (ep 20) and arriving with them at the end (ep 22). Under the green basement light her white clothes and bow read mint, under red light her hair reads red-brown
- Tell apart from: Riko Amanai (similar dark hair, but a sailor uniform and white headband); Misato Kuroi (black maid dress); Mei Mei (silver hair, purple dress); Ogami's schoolgirl disguise (long black hair, but a white shirt, sweater vest and ribbon tie); Nobuko Takada (long black twin tails, no scar, idol clothes)

### Mei Mei
- Hair: pale silver-blue, in one long thick braid that hangs over a shoulder or down her back, with long bangs falling over one eye; in tight close-ups loose strands hang over her face and the braid may be out of frame. Loose to the waist, unbraided, in a hotel (ep 22). Under warm light it reads cream-blonde; under station or tunnel light it reads mint, cyan or turquoise like Ui Ui's
- Eyes: lilac, narrowed or closed in a smile, glowing white when she attacks; long lashes
- Outfit: dark purple long-sleeved dress to the calf with puffed shoulders and a high ribbed collar, baggy dark purple pants tucked into brown boots; purple nails and lipstick. Playing ping-pong with Todo (ep 6): white polo with lavender trim, short black skirt with a purple side stripe, white sneakers. In a Kuala Lumpur hotel (ep 22): under a lavender sheet in bed, then a cream bathrobe, a white bandage on her left cheek
- Accessories: a huge battle axe (wooden haft, broad engraved silver blade), carried wrapped in dark red cloth by Ui Ui in ep 8; black crows (a crow outlined in cyan for Bird Strike); a small square earring; a phone in a red case; usually next to her little brother Ui Ui
- Other forms: in Gakuganji's sunset-lit office and at ping-pong with Todo in a wooden gym (ep 6); with Ui Ui and Yuji at a cemetery at night and in Meiji-Jingumae Station (ep 8); in a subway tunnel (eps 9, 10, 12); inside a dark wooden coffin and fighting the Smallpox Deity in its graveyard domain, facing Kenjaku (eps 12, 14); imagined dead, upside down in red light (ep 14); in bed with Ui Ui in a Kuala Lumpur hotel (ep 22); chibi panels; a silhouette with her axe in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (short spiky white hair, male); Uraume (white chin-length bob, monk robes); Ui Ui (short hair, small boy); Kasumi Miwa (straight blunt bangs across the forehead, white shirt and tie); Mahito (stitched face); Yuki Tsukumo (very long blonde hair, sleeveless top and jeans)
