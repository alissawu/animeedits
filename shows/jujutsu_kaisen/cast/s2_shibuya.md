# Jujutsu Kaisen season 2, Shibuya Incident arc (MAPPA, 2023)

## Cast

Lighting: most of these episodes happen at night, underground in Shibuya Station (eps 9–10) or on dark rooftops and streets (eps 10–11), under washed-out blue-white or teal light that makes skin pale and turns white hair and white clothes pale blue (Gojo's hair, Jogo's grey skin, Ogami's white haori, Awasaka's white shirt, the grandson's sweater). Other scenes are strongly tinted: the opening song is drenched in red and mostly silhouettes; Gojo's memories of his school years (ep 9) are bright, soft and overexposed; the ep 11 flashbacks are warm sepia (an apartment, a diner, a park) or dark blue (a forest at night); Mechamaru's hidden camera (ep 10) is green-grey with scan lines and static; the ending song is sunny daytime Shibuya with a faded film-camera look; the next-episode previews are white line drawings on black. Under tinted light, match on shape instead of color: hairstyle, outfit cut, scars and accessories.

Setting: Halloween night, October 31, 2018, in and around Shibuya. Episode numbers are season 2's (ep 9, 10, 11 are episodes 33, 34, 35 overall). Gojo, Shoko, Nanami, Mei Mei and Utahime are adults, and a man who looks like adult Geto is Kenjaku. Ep 9: Gojo fights Jogo, Hanami, Choso and Mahito on the B5F subway platform among crowds of civilians, and Kenjaku seals him; the cold open shows Yuji, Megumi, Nobara, Maki, Panda, Toge, Nanami and Todo; teenage Gojo and Geto, Riko Amanai, Misato Kuroi and the Time Vessel Association appear only in Gojo's memories, and Yuta and Rika only while Kenjaku talks about them. Ep 10: Mei Mei, Ui Ui, Mechamaru's communicator and Niji Ebina in a subway tunnel; the three sorcerer teams (Naobito, Maki and Nobara; Kusakabe and Panda; Nanami, Megumi and Ino); Ijichi, Haruta Shigemo and Uraume; the Hasaba twins with Kenjaku; Kasumi Miwa and Sukuna only in one imagined shot each; Ogami, her grandson and Awasaka only after the ending song. Ep 11: Yuji, Megumi and Ino fight Awasaka, Ogami and her grandson at Shibuya Central Tower; Toji appears only as the grandson transformed by Ogami's séance; flashbacks show Ino eating with Nanami in a warm-lit restaurant, Gojo as a baby and a child, and Ogami and Awasaka when younger, with the people they killed (unnamed civilians, such as a businessman in a white shirt in an apartment); Kenjaku, the curses, Mei Mei, Uraume and the Hasaba twins are not on screen in ep 11 except in the opening song. Hanami dies in ep 9. The opening song (all three episodes) shows most of the cast, including Sukuna, Shoko, Yaga and Nitta; the ending song shows Yuji, Megumi and Nobara in daytime Shibuya. The crowds of civilians, many in Halloween costumes (clowns, the Joker, angels, nuns, maids, witches, animal ears), are unnamed and are not any of the characters below.

### Satoru Gojo
- Hair: snow-white, spiky and messy. With the black blindfold on (opening song) it is pushed straight up into spikes; with it off (eps 9–10) it falls loose over his forehead and eyes in messy spikes. As a student (memories, ep 9), and as a baby or child (ep 11 flashbacks), the same white hair falling loose
- Eyes: vivid glowing cyan-blue ("Six Eyes") with long white eyelashes; uncovered almost the whole time in eps 9–10 and often in extreme close-up. Otherwise hidden behind the blindfold (adult) or small round sunglasses (student)
- Outfit: as an adult: dark navy-black long jacket with a high collar zipped up to the chin, black pants. As a student (memories, ep 9): navy Jujutsu High uniform with a high collar and one gold swirl button, slim straight pants; tall and lean
- Accessories: adult: wide black cloth blindfold, off in eps 9–10; blood smeared under his left eye and down his cheek from midway through ep 9 into ep 10 (also in the opening song). Student: small round sunglasses with thin silver wire frames and dark lenses
- Other forms: bound by the Prison Realm (end of ep 9, start of ep 10): dark red fleshy tendrils with eyeballs wrap around him and pin him, then shrink into a small reddish cube covered in eyes; inside the Prison Realm (ep 10), alone in darkness, eyes closed. Fights in a blur of cyan light streaks; his domain Unlimited Void flashes as a starry cosmic void (ep 9). In his memories (ep 9), the bloodied student carrying Riko's body wrapped in a sheet through a clapping crowd. Baby (ep 11 flashback): newborn with white hair and white eyelashes, swaddled in white, pale washed-out frame. Child (ep 11 flashback): blue-grey hoodie, black track pants with a white side stripe, extreme close-ups of a glowing blue iris. In the opening song he pulls the blindfold down to show one blue eye
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto (same student uniform, but black hair in a bun, baggy pants and earrings); Mahito (long, straight grey-blue hair hanging past the shoulders, stitched seams across the face and neck, one blue and one grey eye that don't glow; Gojo's hair is short and spiky, his eyes glow cyan, and his only face mark is the blood smear under his left eye); Mei Mei and Uraume (also white or silver hair, but a long braid or ponytail, or a blunt chin-length bob with monk robes); Naobito Zenin (white slicked-back hair on an old man with a mustache); Toge Inumaki (platinum hair, but his collar covers his mouth); Ui Ui (teal hair, small boy in suspenders). Under red light (opening song) his hair reads pink; it is still long and spiky, unlike Yuji's short pink undercut with its red hood. Gojo never wears a red hood or a red scarf: the figure seen from behind in a long navy coat with a red hood at the neck in the sunny ending song is Yuji, whose short hair looks pale against the sky. Student vs adult: sunglasses with the student uniform vs blindfold

### Suguru Geto
- Hair: black with a blue-green sheen; as a student, short, pulled up into a small bun on the crown, with one long bang hanging down one side of his face
- Eyes: narrow, dark, heavy-lidded, often smiling
- Outfit: as a student: the same navy high-collared uniform jacket with a gold swirl button as Gojo, but with very wide, baggy pants gathered at the ankle; broad-shouldered
- Accessories: large dark navy gauge earrings in stretched earlobes (visible in almost every close-up)
- Other forms: appears only as a teenager in Gojo's memories (ep 9): the student with a bun and earrings leaning in beside young Gojo in a classroom, laughing with him, standing alone under trees. In 2018 the man with his face is Kenjaku
- Tell apart from: Kenjaku (same face, but long loose hair, priest robes and a stitched scar across the forehead; 2018); Satoru Gojo (white hair, sunglasses); Toji Fushiguro (black hair falling flat, no bun or earrings, scar at the mouth); Choso (two short tufts, blood stripe across the nose); Yuta Okkotsu (short messy black hair, white uniform)

### Kenjaku (in Suguru Geto's body)
- Hair: long, straight black hair flowing past the shoulders, the upper part tied in a bun on the crown, one long bang down the left side of the face
- Eyes: narrow, golden-brown, smug half-lidded and amused look
- Outfit: Buddhist priest robes: dark purple-black kimono and hakama with wide sleeves and a white under-collar, a dark green patchwork kasaya with gold borders and a gold strap over the left shoulder, white tabi socks and zori sandals. In the mahjong flashback (ep 9): a beige zip-up jacket with a high collar
- Accessories: a thick horizontal line of surgical stitches across the forehead (the key identifier); large dark gauge earrings. Holds the Prison Realm, a small dark red fleshy cube with eyes on its faces (eps 9–10)
- Other forms: lifts off the top of his skull to show a brain with teeth (ep 9); his own right hand grabs his throat and chokes him (ep 10). Close-ups that crop out his forehead (ep 9) still show the long hair, single bang and gauge earring. In ep 11 only a red-lit glimpse in the opening song (facing Gojo); not otherwise on screen
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto as a student (short hair in a bun, navy uniform, no stitches; ep 9 memories only) and Suguru Geto in priest robes (ep 5 cult takeover: same kasaya, but younger, no forehead stitches; not in these episodes); Choso (spiky hair with two tufts, black bar across the nose, purple scarf); Aoi Todo (top-knot bun, but a scarred face and a huge build); Mimiko Hasaba (long dark hair, but a schoolgirl with bangs)

### Shoko Ieiri
- Hair: brown. As an adult, long, straight and past the shoulders. As a student, a short chin-length bob with bangs swept to one side
- Eyes: brown, slightly sleepy, with a small mole under one eye; as an adult, dark tired lines under the eyes
- Outfit: as an adult: white lab coat over a blue-teal turtleneck, navy cropped pants and beige heels. As a student: navy Jujutsu High jacket with a matching short pencil skirt, grey-brown tights and brown loafers
- Accessories: cigarette, or a lollipop stick in her mouth
- Other forms: the adult appears only briefly in the opening song (all three episodes)
- Tell apart from: Nobara Kugisaki (also a brown bob, but orange-brown and with a tool belt and hammer; 2018 setting with Yuji and Megumi); Mimiko Hasaba (brown hair but a sailor uniform); Utahime Iori (black hair, shrine-maiden clothes)

### Utahime Iori
- Hair: long blackish-purple hair with blunt straight-cut bangs; two front locks hang over the shoulders, each bound with red-and-black ties
- Eyes: brown, often scowling or comically angry
- Outfit: shrine-maiden (miko) outfit: white wide-sleeved top, red pleated hakama with a red bow, brown boots
- Accessories: none notable; as an adult, a scar across the nose and right cheek
- Other forms: not seen in eps 9–11. The 2006 version (eps 1 and 3) has no scar
- Tell apart from: Riko Amanai (similar dark hair, but a sailor uniform and white headband); Misato Kuroi (black maid dress); Mei Mei (silver hair, black suit); Ogami's schoolgirl disguise (long black hair, but a white shirt, sweater vest and ribbon tie)

### Mei Mei
- Hair: pale silver-blue. As an adult (2018), one thick braid hanging down over the front of her face, covering one eye; in tight close-ups loose strands hang over her face and the braid may be out of frame. In 2006 (not in these episodes), a high ponytail with two long front locks
- Eyes: lilac, narrowed or closed in a smile; long lashes
- Outfit: dark purple long-sleeved dress with puffed shoulders, baggy purple pants tucked into light brown boots; purple nails
- Accessories: a huge battle axe and crows; a small square earring; usually next to her little brother Ui Ui
- Other forms: in a subway tunnel with Ui Ui and Yuji (end of ep 9, ep 10); a chibi comedy panel with Ui Ui and Yuji (ep 10); a silhouette with her axe in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (short spiky white hair, male); Uraume (white chin-length bob, monk robes); Ui Ui (short hair, small boy); Kasumi Miwa (straight blunt bangs across the forehead, smiling with eyes closed, white shirt and tie, only in one bright imagined shot in ep 10); Mahito (stitched face)

### Masamichi Yaga

### Toji Fushiguro
- Hair: straight black, messy, falling over the forehead and to the ears
- Eyes: narrow, dark, lazy and smirking (green per the wiki); summoned in ep 11 night scenes, they glow pale blue
- Outfit: very muscular build. Summoned in ep 11: on Ogami's grandson's body, a loose light-blue or white knit sweater with a ribbed collar, black pants and dark sneakers. In his own body (eps 1–5, not in these episodes): tight black short-sleeved shirt and very wide white baggy pants
- Accessories: a scar at the right corner of the mouth (key)
- Other forms: ep 11 only: Toji's face and black hair appearing on the grandson's body after the séance; a glowing, cracked, half-transformed face mid-transformation
- Tell apart from: Megumi Fushiguro (hair spikes outward, school uniform, no scar); Suguru Geto (bun and earrings); Shiu Kong (suit); Ogami's grandson before the séance (olive-green mohawk, his own face); Yuta Okkotsu (white uniform, no scar)

### Megumi Fushiguro
- Hair: black, spiky, sticking out in every direction like a sea urchin

- Tell apart from: Toji Fushiguro (hair falls flat over the forehead, scar at the mouth, adult build); Yuji Itadori (pink hair); Yu Haibara (bowl cut); Satoru Gojo (white hair); Choso (two hair tufts, nose marking, purple scarf); Takuma Ino (black beanie); Yuta Okkotsu (softer black hair, white uniform)

### Yuji Itadori
- Hair: short, spiky pink hair with darker undercut sides
- Eyes: light brown; a thin closed slit under each eye (Sukuna's second pair of eyes)
- Outfit: dark navy high-collared Jujutsu High jacket with gold buttons and a red hood worn bunched around the neck like a scarf, dark pants, red sneakers; muscular

- Eyes: pale blue
- Outfit: black sweatshirt with a wide collar, black pants, navy high-top sneakers
- Accessories: pulls the beanie down into a full black balaclava that shows only his light-blue eyes. Summons a glowing yellow-green spiral drill (Kaichi) and a black liquid blob he rides (Reiki)

Ep 11: holding a talisman-wrapped stake on the tower, pulling the mask down over his face, beaten bloody by Toji with the mask pulled off, and in a warm-lit restaurant flashback with Nanami (the beanie looks dark brown there, with a black T-shirt). ### Jiro Awasaka
- Hair: mostly bald, apart from one curled dark tuft sticking up on top; a dark goatee tuft on the chin
- Eyes: huge, round, staring; thick dark eyebrows; big handlebar mustache
- Outfit: short, stocky, very muscular old man; white henley T-shirt (pale blue under night light) tucked into a tall tan ribbed belly band, black athletic shorts, white sock-like tabi shoes
- Accessories: a knife tucked into the belly band
- Other forms: after the ending song of ep 10, on a rooftop with Ogami and her grandson. Young (ep 11 flashback): full head of black hair, stubble beard, navy hoodie, in a dark blue forest. Middle-aged (ep 11 flashbacks): greying spiky hair, stubble, deeply lined face, tan jacket, crouched on a balcony railing above child Gojo, or pedalling a swan boat. Later a bruise on the forehead, then a bloodied mouth after Yuji's punch
- Tell apart from: Masamichi Yaga (has hair, track jacket); Naobito Zenin (slicked white hair, kimono); the elderly curse user from ep 2 (glasses, head wrap); Niji Ebina (green hood, no lips); Atsuya Kusakabe (spiky brown hair, younger, lollipop; the man in a tan jacket in the ep 11 flashbacks with a lined face and greying hair is Awasaka); the businessman in a white shirt with slicked dark hair, drinking on a white sofa in Ogami's apartment flashback (ep 11), who is an unnamed victim, not Awasaka

### Ogami (Granny Ogami)
- Hair: grey-white hair in a large rounded bun with a small gold ornament
- Eyes: almost always shut; deeply wrinkled tiny old face
- Outfit: white haori (pale blue under night light) with red stripes down the front edges, over a dark green kimono with an olive sash; zori sandals
- Accessories: a long string of dark prayer beads, hands pressed together in prayer
- Other forms: after the ending song of ep 10, on a rooftop with Awasaka and her grandson. Ep 11 flashback disguise as a high-school girl: long straight black hair, white shirt, maroon-brown sweater vest, red ribbon tie, dark pleated skirt, black socks; the disguise slips into a half-old, half-young face and then her old face on the schoolgirl uniform. In civilian flashbacks, a pink blouse in a park and a diner
- Tell apart from: Riko Amanai (the disguise has no headband and wears a vest and ribbon instead of a sailor collar); Mimiko Hasaba (navy sailor uniform, ep 10 only); Rika Orimoto (a small girl in a navy dress with a red bow); Naobito Zenin (old man, mustache)

### Ogami's Grandson

- Eyes: wide, blank, staring; often closed
- Outfit: tall and thin with a long neck; loose light-blue or white knit sweater with a ribbed collar, black pants, dark sneakers

- Other forms: after the ending song of ep 10, beside Ogami. After the séance (ep 11), his face and hair become Toji Fushiguro's (black messy hair, scar at the mouth, smirk) but he keeps the same sweater
- Tell apart from: Toji in eps 1–5 (black T-shirt and white baggy pants); Takuma Ino (beanie, all black); Kento Nanami (blond, suit, goggles); Haruta Shigemo (blond ponytail); Niji Ebina (green frog-like hood with eyes, shirtless)

### Jogo
- Hair: none; the head is shaped like a volcano with a brown rocky crater on top, sometimes erupting in flame
- Eyes: one large single eye with a red iris; grey-white skin (pale blue under the station lights); black teeth; corks plugging the ear holes on the sides of his head
- Outfit: short and hunched; loose black clothes under a yellow-green cape with black leopard spots and a fluffy white collar
- Accessories: flames erupt from his head when he is angry; fire from his hands
- Other forms: his right arm torn off by Gojo (ep 9); at the mahjong table (ep 9); squatting on rocks by a misty hot spring in a memory (ep 10). In ep 11 only a red-lit glimpse of the spotted cape in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Hanami (tall, branches for eyes); Dagon (red octopus); transfigured humans (also grey-blue and deformed, but no volcano head, cape or single eye)

### Hanami
- Hair: none; a smooth, pale helmet-like head with exposed teeth and two wooden branches sprouting from the eye sockets
- Eyes: no eyes, only the branches; black zigzag lines down the face; a wide grin of bare teeth
- Outfit: very tall and muscular, pale beige body covered in black vein-like lines; white cloth draped over the left arm and shoulder, black baggy pants with a white sash, bare clawed feet
- Accessories: a big red flower on the left shoulder; roots and wooden attacks
- Other forms: roots and branches bursting from the floor; crushed to dust against a wall (ep 9); seated at the table in the mahjong flashback (ep 9). Dead after ep 9 except in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Jogo (volcano head, spotted cape); transfigured humans (deformed grey, blue or pink bodies, no branches or flower)

### Choso
- Hair: spiky dark brown hair tied into two short tufts sticking up and out on top
- Eyes: small, dark, tired and half-closed, with purple eyeshadow; a thick black blood stripe across the bridge of the nose from cheek to cheek
- Outfit: loose white robe and baggy white pants under a purple vest with dark vertical stripes and a large purple scarf wrapped high around the neck, brown boots
- Accessories: presses his palms together to fire blood; controls red blood as whips or orbs
- Other forms: on the B5F platform in eps 9–10; in ep 11 only in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto / Kenjaku (one bun, earrings, long hair, priest robes); Toji (no tufts); Megumi Fushiguro (spiky black hair, but a school uniform and no face marking)

### Mahito
- Hair: long, pale grey-blue, hanging past the shoulders in a few loose, tied strands
- Eyes: one dark blue, one grey, not glowing; stitch lines and patchwork seams across the face, neck and body, one running straight across his nose and cheeks
- Outfit: black shawl-like long-sleeved top with a faint grid pattern and slit sleeves, black pants, white shoes or barefoot
- Accessories: reshapes his own body (blades, extra hands, a pale branching mass); the deformed transfigured humans are his work
- Other forms: splits into copies of himself (ep 9). In the mahjong flashback (ep 9, outdoors at dusk) his hair reads silver-grey. In a misty hot-spring memory (ep 10) he floats face-up in the steaming water, eyes closed, while Jogo squats on the rocks. In ep 11 only in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (short spiky white hair, glowing cyan eyes with white lashes, a blood smear only under his left eye, no stitches, high-collared jacket); Kenjaku (stitches only across the forehead, black hair); Uraume (white bob); Mei Mei (pale blue braid over her face, purple dress); Ui Ui (short teal hair, small boy in white); Kasumi Miwa (long light-blue hair with blunt bangs)

### Dagon
- Hair: none; a round, bright-red octopus-like head with striped tentacles around the mouth
- Eyes: two big round white eyes (blank green in the evolved form)
- Outfit: unevolved (eps 9–10): a bulky red body with a white cloth draped over the head and back like a hood. Evolved (opening song only): a tall red-and-tan humanoid with small wings at the lower back
- Accessories: none
- Other forms: stands near Kenjaku and the curses on B5F (eps 9–10); in ep 11 only in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Jogo (volcano head, one eye, spotted cape); Mimiko Hasaba's doll (also a small red octopus-like thing, but a stitched plush with blue button eyes and a rope noose, held low beside the twins)
