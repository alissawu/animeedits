# Jujutsu Kaisen season 2, Shibuya Incident arc (MAPPA, 2023)

## Cast

Lighting: most of these episodes happen at night, underground in Shibuya Station (eps 9–10) or on dark rooftops and streets (eps 10–11), under washed-out blue-white or teal light that makes skin pale and turns white hair and white clothes pale blue (Gojo's hair, Jogo's grey skin, Ogami's white haori, Awasaka's white shirt, the grandson's sweater). Other scenes are strongly tinted: the opening song is drenched in red and mostly silhouettes; Gojo's memories of his school years (ep 9) are bright, soft and overexposed; the ep 11 flashbacks are warm sepia (an apartment, a diner, a park) or dark blue (a forest at night); Mechamaru's hidden camera (ep 10) is green-grey with scan lines and static; the ending song is sunny daytime Shibuya with a faded film-camera look; the next-episode previews are white line drawings on black. Under tinted light, match on shape instead of color: hairstyle, outfit cut, scars and accessories.

Setting: Halloween night, October 31, 2018, in and around Shibuya. Episode numbers are season 2's (ep 9, 10, 11 are episodes 33, 34, 35 overall). Gojo, Shoko, Nanami, Mei Mei and Utahime are adults, and a man who looks like adult Geto is Kenjaku. Ep 9: Gojo fights Jogo, Hanami, Choso and Mahito on the B5F subway platform among crowds of civilians, and Kenjaku seals him; the cold open shows Yuji, Megumi, Nobara, Maki, Panda, Toge, Nanami and Todo; teenage Gojo and Geto, Riko Amanai, Misato Kuroi and the Time Vessel Association appear only in Gojo's memories, and Yuta and Rika only while Kenjaku talks about them. Ep 10: Mei Mei, Ui Ui, Mechamaru's communicator and Niji Ebina in a subway tunnel; the three sorcerer teams (Naobito, Maki and Nobara; Kusakabe and Panda; Nanami, Megumi and Ino); Ijichi, Haruta Shigemo and Uraume; the Hasaba twins with Kenjaku; Kasumi Miwa and Sukuna only in one imagined shot each; Ogami, her grandson and Awasaka only after the ending song. Ep 11: Yuji, Megumi and Ino fight Awasaka, Ogami and her grandson at Shibuya Central Tower; Toji appears only as the grandson transformed by Ogami's séance; flashbacks show Ino eating with Nanami in a warm-lit restaurant, Gojo as a baby and a child, and Ogami and Awasaka when younger, with the people they killed (unnamed civilians, such as a businessman in a white shirt in an apartment); Kenjaku, the curses, Mei Mei, Uraume and the Hasaba twins are not on screen in ep 11 except in the opening song. Hanami dies in ep 9. The opening song (all three episodes) shows most of the cast, including Sukuna, Shoko, Yaga and Nitta; the ending song shows Yuji, Megumi and Nobara in daytime Shibuya. The crowds of civilians, many in Halloween costumes (clowns, the Joker, angels, nuns, maids, witches, animal ears), are unnamed and are not any of the characters below.

### Satoru Gojo
- Hair: snow-white, spiky and messy. With the black blindfold on (opening song) it is pushed straight up into spikes; with it off (eps 9–10) it falls loose over his forehead and eyes in messy spikes. As a student (memories, ep 9), and as a baby or child (ep 11 flashbacks), the same white hair falling loose
- Eyes: vivid glowing cyan-blue ("Six Eyes") with long white eyelashes; uncovered almost the whole time in eps 9–10 and often in extreme close-up. Otherwise hidden behind the blindfold (adult) or small round sunglasses (student)
- Outfit: as an adult: dark navy-black long jacket with a high collar zipped up to the chin, black pants. As a student (memories, ep 9): navy Jujutsu High uniform with a high collar and one gold swirl button, slim straight pants; tall and lean
- Accessories: adult: wide black cloth blindfold, off in eps 9–10; blood smeared under his left eye and down his cheek from midway through ep 9 into ep 10 (also in the opening song). Student: small round sunglasses with thin silver wire frames and dark lenses
- Other forms: bound by the Prison Realm (end of ep 9, start of ep 10): dark red fleshy tendrils with eyeballs wrap around him and pin him, then shrink into a small reddish cube covered in eyes; inside the Prison Realm (ep 10), alone in darkness, eyes closed. Fights in a blur of cyan light streaks; his domain Unlimited Void flashes as a starry cosmic void (ep 9). In his memories (ep 9), the bloodied student carrying Riko's body wrapped in a sheet through a clapping crowd. Baby (ep 11 flashback): newborn with white hair and white eyelashes, swaddled in white, pale washed-out frame. Child (ep 11 flashback): blue-grey hoodie, black track pants with a white side stripe, extreme close-ups of a glowing blue iris. In the opening song he pulls the blindfold down to show one blue eye
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto (same student uniform, but black hair in a bun, baggy pants and earrings); Mahito (long, straight grey-blue hair hanging past the shoulders, stitched seams across the face and neck, one blue and one grey eye that don't glow; Gojo's hair is short and spiky, his eyes glow cyan, and his only face mark is the blood smear under his left eye); Mei Mei and Uraume (also white or silver hair, but a long braid or ponytail, or a blunt chin-length bob with monk robes); Naobito Zenin (white slicked-back hair on an old man with a mustache); Toge Inumaki (platinum hair, but his collar covers his mouth); Ui Ui (teal hair, small boy in suspenders). Under red light (opening song) his hair reads pink; it is still long and spiky, unlike Yuji's short pink undercut with its red hood. Gojo never wears a red hood or a red scarf: the figure seen from behind in a long navy coat with a red hood at the neck in the sunny ending song is Yuji, whose short hair looks pale against the sky. Student vs adult: sunglasses with the student uniform vs blindfold

### Suguru Geto
- Hair: black with a blue-green sheen; as a student, short, pulled up into a small bun on the crown, with one long bang hanging down one side of his face
- Eyes: narrow, dark, heavy-lidded, often smiling
- Outfit: as a student: the same navy high-collared uniform jacket with a gold swirl button as Gojo, but with very wide, baggy pants gathered at the ankle; broad-shouldered
- Accessories: large dark navy gauge earrings in stretched earlobes (visible in almost every close-up)
- Other forms: appears only as a teenager in Gojo's memories (ep 9): the student with a bun and earrings leaning in beside young Gojo in a classroom, laughing with him, standing alone under trees. In 2018 the man with his face is Kenjaku
- Tell apart from: Kenjaku (same face, but long loose hair, priest robes and a stitched scar across the forehead; 2018); Satoru Gojo (white hair, sunglasses); Toji Fushiguro (black hair falling flat, no bun or earrings, scar at the mouth); Choso (two short tufts, blood stripe across the nose); Yuta Okkotsu (short messy black hair, white uniform)

### Kenjaku (in Suguru Geto's body)
- Hair: long, straight black hair flowing past the shoulders, the upper part tied in a bun on the crown, one long bang down the left side of the face
- Eyes: narrow, golden-brown, smug half-lidded and amused look
- Outfit: Buddhist priest robes: dark purple-black kimono and hakama with wide sleeves and a white under-collar, a dark green patchwork kasaya with gold borders and a gold strap over the left shoulder, white tabi socks and zori sandals. In the mahjong flashback (ep 9): a beige zip-up jacket with a high collar
- Accessories: a thick horizontal line of surgical stitches across the forehead (the key identifier); large dark gauge earrings. Holds the Prison Realm, a small dark red fleshy cube with eyes on its faces (eps 9–10)
- Other forms: lifts off the top of his skull to show a brain with teeth (ep 9); his own right hand grabs his throat and chokes him (ep 10). Close-ups that crop out his forehead (ep 9) still show the long hair, single bang and gauge earring. In ep 11 only a red-lit glimpse in the opening song (facing Gojo); not otherwise on screen
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto as a student (short hair in a bun, navy uniform, no stitches; ep 9 memories only) and Suguru Geto in priest robes (ep 5 cult takeover: same kasaya, but younger, no forehead stitches; not in these episodes); Choso (spiky hair with two tufts, black bar across the nose, purple scarf); Aoi Todo (top-knot bun, but a scarred face and a huge build); Mimiko Hasaba (long dark hair, but a schoolgirl with bangs)

### Utahime Iori

### Mei Mei
- Hair: pale silver-blue, in one long thick braid that hangs over a shoulder or down her back, with long bangs falling over one eye; in tight close-ups loose strands hang over her face and the braid may be out of frame. Under warm sunset light it reads cream-blonde
- Eyes: lilac, narrowed or closed in a smile; long lashes
- Outfit: dark purple long-sleeved dress with puffed shoulders and a high ribbed collar, baggy purple pants tucked into light brown boots; purple nails and lipstick. Playing ping-pong with Todo (ep 6): white polo with lavender trim, short black skirt with a purple side stripe, white sneakers
- Accessories: a huge battle axe and crows; a small square earring; usually next to her little brother Ui Ui
- Other forms: in Gakuganji's sunset-lit office and at ping-pong with Todo in a wooden gym (ep 6); in a subway tunnel with Ui Ui and Yuji (end of ep 9, ep 10); a chibi comedy panel with Ui Ui and Yuji (ep 10); a silhouette with her axe in the opening song
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (short spiky white hair, male); Uraume (white chin-length bob, monk robes); Ui Ui (short hair, small boy); Kasumi Miwa (straight blunt bangs across the forehead, smiling with eyes closed, white shirt and tie, only in one bright imagined shot in ep 10); Mahito (stitched face)
