# Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (MAPPA, 2021 film)

## Cast

Lighting: some scenes are strongly tinted (red, blue, amber or sepia), which shifts hair and clothing colors. Under tinted light, match on shape instead: hairstyle, outfit cut and accessories.

### Yuta Okkotsu
- Hair: black, short, messy and spiky, with strands hanging into his eyes. In the post-credits scene (a year later) it is longer and smoother, parted at the side.
- Eyes: dark gray-black. Heavy dark circles in the first act, wide and often tearful.
- Outfit: Jujutsu High uniform in WHITE (the only white uniform in the film). It has a high stand collar, an off-center button line with one gold button, and sleeves pushed up to the forearms, with a white drawstring at the waist. He wears it with slim black pants and white high-top sneakers. Alternates:
  - Old high school (opening): white dress shirt with rolled sleeves, loose dark tie, dark pants, no blazer.
  - Isolation cell: white top, dark pants, barefoot, hunched on a wooden chair in a room hung with ropes and paper talismans.
  - Training: navy T-shirt and dark track pants. Casual: plain white T-shirt.
  - December scenes: turquoise/teal scarf over the white uniform.
  - Snowy epilogue: long black coat with the teal scarf.
- Accessories: long dark purple/indigo sword bag slung across his back, and a katana with a red-wrapped hilt. Rika's silver ring is on his finger (too small to see in thumbnails).
- Other forms: as a small child in flashbacks:
  - Hospital: light-green hospital gown with an IV stand.
  - Playground: light-blue long-sleeve shirt.
  - Ring scene and Rika's death: yellow hoodie and purple backpack.

  In the climax he is often framed inside or in front of Rika's giant white body.
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto and other black-haired men. Yuta's hair is short and he wears the white uniform; no one else wears white.

### Rika Orimoto
- Hair: (human girl) long, straight, past the shoulders, with bangs. Drawn dark brown in the film (the wiki says black).
- Eyes: (human girl) brown, soft. Small beauty mark on her chin.
- Outfit: (human girl, about 11) sleeveless navy-blue dress. It has a white collar edged with small dark hearts and a red ribbon bow at the throat; she wears purple flat shoes. Alternates:
  - Playground flashback: white long-sleeve blouse under the navy dress.
  - Hospital flashback: pale gray cardigan over pink plaid pajamas.
- Accessories: the ring. The engagement-ring promise scene is in a sandbox.
- Other forms: CURSED SPIRIT "Rika" (most of her screen time):
  - Body: a huge pale white/light-gray monster with black speckles. She has a skeletal, rib-ridged torso and very long thin arms ending in long claws. Her lower body trails off into a black smoky tail; she has no legs.
  - Head: a ridged white shell like a clam or brain, with thick spotted tendrils like dreadlocks. A huge vertical mouth of needle teeth with a pink tongue fills the front of the head.
  - At full power (climax), the shell splits open to show a single cyclops eye with a red iris, and Yuta stands inside her open ribcage.
  - Partial manifestations: only giant white clawed hands and arms around Yuta, or a toothy mouth rising from a pool of blood in red-tinted shots.
  - Freed at the end: the human girl again, in the navy dress, surrounded by glowing particles.
- Tell apart from: Geto's curses. Rika is the only white-and-black curse with a shell-shaped head and a tooth-lined vertical mouth; Geto's are multicolored, and his pelican curse is white but has feathers and a beak. The human girl vs. young Mimiko and Nanako: Rika wears the navy dress with the white heart collar and red bow.

### Satoru Gojo
- Hair: white, with a faint lavender tint in the shading. With the eye bandage on, it is pushed up into tall spikes. With the bandage off, it falls flat and soft over his forehead.
- Eyes: vivid glowing blue (the Six Eyes). Usually hidden.
- Outfit: dark navy-black, almost purple, high-collared long zip jacket with a huge folded collar, slim black pants and black dress shoes. Alternates:
  - Casual (meeting the higher-ups, on the phone): hair down, small round dark sunglasses, dark olive/black zip hoodie over a black T-shirt.
  - Snowy epilogue: long black coat.
  - Post-credits (Kenya): hair down, small round light-blue-tinted sunglasses, white shirt.
- Accessories: in this film he wears WHITE BANDAGES wrapped several times around his eyes, in loose layered strips. He never wears the black blindfold in the main film. He peels the bandage up to show one blue eye against Miguel. It comes off entirely for the final alley scene with Geto and the ending with Yuta.
- Other forms: TEEN Gojo in brief flashbacks: black school uniform and tiny round dark sunglasses, laughing next to teen Geto.
- Tell apart from: Toge Inumaki, who has the other light spiky hair. Gojo is far taller, has bandaged eyes and wears the high-collared jacket, while Toge's mouth is hidden by a knit collar.

### Suguru Geto
- Hair: long black hair with a blue-green sheen, in a half-up topknot bun. The rest flows loose to mid-back, and one long strand of fringe hangs down his forehead.
- Eyes: narrow, slit-like and usually smiling. They read as dark on screen (the wiki says purple).
- Outfit: Buddhist priest robes:
  - Under-robes: dark navy/indigo kimono with very wide sleeves and loose hakama-style trousers, and a white under-collar at the neck.
  - Kasaya: green panels in a gold/ochre lattice border, worn diagonally across his body and tied with a gold cord.
  - Footwear: white tabi socks and zori sandals with red thongs.
- Accessories: large black ball-shaped gauge earrings in stretched earlobes. He wields Playful Cloud (a red three-section staff), and is sometimes shown holding a dark curse orb.
- Other forms:
  - Wounded in the final fight: hair loosened, face bloodied, slumped against an alley wall at dusk. His right arm is missing.
  - Flashbacks (TEEN Geto): black school uniform with hair in a shorter bun and the same fringe and earrings. In the village flashback that sets up the twins' rescue he wears a white collared shirt, with a blood spot on his face (sepia-toned). In the 2007 Shinjuku crowd flashback his long hair is half-down and he is in plain black.
- Tell apart from:
  - Noritoshi Kamo: also long black hair, but Kamo's side locks are bound in white wrappings and he wears the dark Kyoto uniform.
  - Aoi Todo: also a topknot, but Todo is shirtless and bulky.
  - Visible difference: Geto's green-and-gold kasaya and black gauge earrings.

### Maki Zenin
- Hair: dark green (the anime color), long, in a high ponytail with loose strands framing one side of her face.
- Eyes: amber/brown, sharp, behind glasses.
- Outfit: dark navy uniform jacket with a high collar, a navy pencil skirt, chunky cream knit slouch leg-warmers and brown boots. Alternates:
  - Training: purple track jacket with a lilac collar, purple shorts or skirt, black knee socks and sneakers.
  - Night of the Parade (vs. Geto): green/teal zip hoodie over a red-and-white horizontally striped shirt, and cream shorts over black leggings.
  - December: magenta/plum striped scarf.
- Accessories: thin rectangular glasses. She carries a long spear or naginata with a red cloth tassel, sometimes in a long red weapon bag.
- Other forms: a chibi gag version in a teal hoodie and striped shirt during the ending gag.
- Tell apart from: Mai Zenin (twin, Kyoto). Maki has glasses and a long ponytail; Mai has a short bob, no glasses and a revolver.

### Toge Inumaki
- Hair: short, spiky, platinum/ash blond.
- Eyes: pale purple-gray, droopy and sleepy-looking.
- Outfit: dark navy uniform. A ribbed teal/blue-green knit turtleneck collar is pulled up over his mouth and nose, so only his eyes show. He wears cropped navy pants and blue shoes. Alternate: navy T-shirt with the same knit collar for training.
- Accessories: a small brown cough-syrup bottle. The snake-and-fang seal is on his tongue and cheeks, but it is rarely visible.
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo, the other white-haired character. Toge is small, has no eye bandage and has his lower face hidden by the teal knit collar.

### Panda
- Hair: black-and-white panda fur. Round black ears and black eye patches on a white face.
- Eyes: small and dark inside the black patches.
- Outfit: none; he is a giant upright panda, much taller and wider than the students. Alternate: in December, a lime-green scarf patterned with tiny panda faces.
- Accessories: teal/blue wristbands on his forearms.
- Other forms: GORILLA MODE against Geto. He becomes hunched and gorilla-shaped, with long shaggy arms, an angular snarling face and glowing red-purple eyes.
- Tell apart from: no one; he is the only animal-shaped ally. Separate him from curses by the clean black-and-white panda markings.

### Masamichi Yaga
- Hair: short spiky dark-brown crop on top with shaved gray sides.
- Eyes: hidden by small tinted (reddish-brown) sunglasses.
- Outfit: dark charcoal suit jacket worn open over a white collarless dress shirt, a brown belt with a metal buckle, and black dress shoes.
- Accessories: sunglasses, mustache and goatee.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Kiyotaka Ijichi (also a suit). Yaga is broad and bearded, has tan skin and wears sunglasses; Ijichi is thin, clean-shaven and wears clear glasses and a tie.

### Shoko Ieiri
- Hair: long, straight, reddish-brown, past the shoulders.
- Eyes: brown with dark under-eye bags (tired look). A beauty mark under one eye.
- Outfit: white lab coat over a blue/teal turtleneck and navy pants.
- Accessories: none signature in this film.
- Other forms: TEEN Shoko in the student flashback: short reddish-brown bob and black school uniform, sitting at a desk with teen Gojo and Geto.
- Tell apart from: Manami Suda (long reddish hair). Shoko wears the white lab coat over a turtleneck; Manami wears a purple sleeveless dress.

### Kiyotaka Ijichi
- Hair: black, mid-length, parted neatly across the forehead.
- Eyes: small, dark, behind black rectangular glasses. Gaunt, worried face.
- Outfit: black suit, white shirt and dark red/maroon tie.
- Accessories: glasses.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Masamichi Yaga, also in a dark suit. Ijichi is thin and wears glasses and a tie; Yaga is bearded and wears sunglasses.

### Akari Nitta
- Hair: blonde chin-length bob with dark brown roots and messy bangs.
- Eyes: pale blue-gray.
- Outfit: dark charcoal suit, white shirt, red tie and brown shoes. She holds a tablet while briefing Gojo by a black car.
- Accessories: none.
- Tell apart from: Ijichi, the other assistant manager in a suit. Nitta is a blonde young woman.

### Miguel
- Hair: hidden under a big puffy white beret.
- Eyes: hidden behind rectangular blue-lensed sunglasses with no arms, in dark frames.
- Outfit: white short-sleeved zip-up top with purple trim at the collar, zipper and sleeves, slim dark navy pants and red shoes. He is tall, very muscular and dark-skinned. He wears the same outfit in the post-credits scene in Kenya.
- Accessories: large gold hoop earrings. He wields the Black Rope, a long dark-red/maroon braided rope that he swings like a whip.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Larue, the other muscular Geto follower. Miguel wears the white beret and blue sunglasses, while Larue is shirtless and blond.

### Larue
- Hair: blond, swept back, held by a thin light-blue headband.
- Eyes: light-colored, often half-closed or squinting.
- Outfit: shirtless and barefoot. Pink heart-shaped marks cover his nipples. Loose purple/violet pants.
- Accessories: blue headband.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Miguel. Larue is pale-skinned, blond and shirtless with the pink hearts; Miguel is dark-skinned with a white beret.

### Nanako Hasaba
- Hair: caramel/honey-blonde in a top bun, with blunt straight bangs.
- Eyes: brown, sharp and sulky.
- Outfit: gyaru schoolgirl look: oversized beige cardigan, white collared shirt, short navy pleated skirt, white slouch socks and brown loafers.
- Accessories: bright lime-green smartphone, which she aims like a camera to use her technique.
- Other forms: as a child in a sepia flashback: short light hair, sleeveless light dress, bruised, inside a wooden cage with her sister.
- Tell apart from: Mimiko, her twin. Nanako has light hair in a bun, a beige cardigan and the green phone; Mimiko has a dark bob and a navy sailor uniform.

### Mimiko Hasaba
- Hair: dark brown bob with blunt straight bangs.
- Eyes: brown, droopy and expressionless.
- Outfit: navy sailor uniform with a white neckerchief, a long navy pleated skirt, gray knee socks and brown loafers.
- Accessories: a pink stitched voodoo-style doll with a yellow rope noose around its neck. She carries the rope as well.
- Other forms: as a child in the sepia flashback: dark hair, sleeveless dress, clutching a doll, bruised, in the cage with her sister.
- Tell apart from: Nanako, her twin (see above). Mimiko vs. young Rika: Mimiko is a teen in a sailor uniform holding a doll.

### Manami Suda
- Hair: long, dusty pink/salmon, past the shoulders, side-swept.
- Eyes: blue-green.
- Outfit: sleeveless, high-necked, tight purple maxi dress with black lace-up open-toe heels. Night Parade: a cream/white coat with a golden-yellow fur collar draped cape-like over the purple dress.
- Accessories: usually holds a tablet, phone or folder (she acts as Geto's assistant).
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Shoko Ieiri. Manami has pink hair and the purple dress; Shoko wears a lab coat.

### Toshihisa Negi
- Hair: spiky brown hair sticking up above a headband.
- Eyes: small, round, wide-eyed and blank. A scar or mark on one cheek.
- Outfit: dark green turtleneck sweater, blue jeans and white shoes.
- Accessories: dark navy headband with stitched lines.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: the other Geto followers. Negi is the only plain-clothed young man (green turtleneck, jeans). He appears only in Geto's group scenes at the temple.

### Mei Mei
- Hair: very pale blue-white/silver. One thick braid hangs down over the front of her face, covering one eye, and a second braid hangs behind.
- Eyes: dark purple, heavy-lidded. Dark purple/red lipstick.
- Outfit: dark purple high-necked, long-sleeved dress with puffed shoulders.
- Accessories: a huge battle axe with a wooden handle, swung one-handed.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Satoru Gojo (white hair). Mei Mei has the long braid over her face and wears purple; Gojo wears bandages and has short spikes.

### Kento Nanami
- Hair: blond, neatly parted and combed.
- Eyes: hidden behind round, goggle-like green-tinted glasses with no arms.
- Outfit: cream/tan suit, teal-blue shirt, and a yellow tie with black leopard spots.
- Accessories: the goggles, the leopard-print tie (he wraps it around his fist) and a cloth-wrapped blunt cleaver.
- Other forms: none. He appears only in Kyoto (amber-tinted shots) and in the sorcerer lineup confronting Geto.
- Tell apart from: Larue (blond). Nanami is fully suited with goggles; Larue is shirtless.

### Takuma Ino
- Hair: brown, shaggy, sticking out from under a beanie.
- Eyes: dark, wide and eager.
- Outfit: dark green-black sweatshirt and baggy black pants.
- Accessories: a black beanie that is actually a rolled-up ski mask.
- Other forms: with the ski mask pulled down, he is a black full-face balaclava with eye holes and a stitched grin.
- Tell apart from: generic sorcerers in dark clothes. Ino is the one in the black beanie.

### Atsuya Kusakabe
- Hair: short, spiky brown.
- Eyes: dark, narrow, scowling.
- Outfit: camel/tan double-breasted trench coat over a dark suit and vest, with a green tie.
- Accessories: a katana.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Kento Nanami (also a light-colored outfit). Kusakabe has brown hair, a camel trench coat and a sword, with no goggles.

### Aoi Todo
- Hair: black, in a top-knot bun with flower-stamen-like strands.
- Eyes: small, dark. A scar on one side of the face.
- Outfit: shirtless in the film; a huge, muscular, tan torso.
- Accessories: none.
- Other forms: none. A brief Kyoto cameo in amber-tinted night streets.
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto (topknot). Todo is shirtless and massively built, with no robes.

### Kasumi Miwa
- Hair: long, straight, pale blue. It looks mint/aqua under the amber Kyoto grade. Side-swept bangs.
- Eyes: blue.
- Outfit: Kyoto uniform: black suit jacket, white shirt, black tie.
- Accessories: a katana with a red-wrapped sheath.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Mei Mei (pale hair). Miwa's hair is loose with no braid, and she wears a black suit with a tie.

### Mai Zenin
- Hair: short dark green/black bob with a longer lock at one side.
- Eyes: amber.
- Outfit: dark Kyoto uniform.
- Accessories: a silver revolver, held out at arm's length.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Maki Zenin. Mai has a short bob, no glasses and a gun.

### Momo Nishimiya
- Hair: blonde twin pigtails tied high, sticking up at the ends.
- Eyes: large, blue-teal.
- Outfit: black dress-style uniform.
- Accessories: a brown leather choker with a pendant, stud earrings and a witch's broom (she rides it).
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Nanako Hasaba (light hair). Momo has twin pigtails and a choker, and wears black, not a cardigan.

### Noritoshi Kamo
- Hair: black, shoulder-length. The two side locks framing his face are bound in white wrappings.
- Eyes: usually closed.
- Outfit: dark Kyoto uniform with light inner sleeves and collar.
- Accessories: bow and arrows.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Suguru Geto. Kamo has the wrapped side locks and closed eyes, and no kasaya or earrings.

### Mechamaru (Ultimate Mechamaru)
- Hair: none; a bald brown metal robot head.
- Eyes: a single green glowing eye-lens.
- Outfit: dark Kyoto uniform with a gray scarf around the neck.
- Accessories: oversized robot hands.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Kyoto curses (the green armored samurai curse). Mechamaru is the robot in a uniform and scarf.

### Tamamo-no-Mae (Geto's curse)
- Hair: very long, straight black hair.
- Eyes: hidden behind a flat white mask-like face with a red-lipped grin.
- Outfit: towering layered red/vermilion court robes with green and pink inner layers (junihitoe style).
- Accessories: none.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: human women. She is twice human height, with a white mask face and red layered robes. She stands beside Geto in the climax.

### Geto's flying curse
- Hair: none; white feathers.
- Eyes: small, beady.
- Outfit: a giant white pelican-like bird with huge wings and a long pale beak with a pouch.
- Accessories: none. Geto and his group ride it to Jujutsu High.
- Other forms: none.
- Tell apart from: Rika. The bird has feathers and a beak, and no teeth or shell head.

### Yuta's bullies
- Hair: the ringleader has short spiky reddish-brown hair, sideburns into a chin-strap beard, and a cut on his forehead. The other three have dark hair.
- Eyes: ordinary, dark.
- Outfit: navy school blazers worn open, white shirts and loose green ties.
- Accessories: the ringleader has silver ear cuffs; one boy holds up a phone.
- Other forms: crushed into a classroom locker by Rika (red-tinted opening).
- Tell apart from: adult sorcerers in suits. The bullies are teens in navy blazers with green ties, in the dusk-lit classroom at the very start.

### Geto's congregation (believers)
- Hair: a bald, heavy middle-aged man; a woman with a brown bob and blunt bangs; an older woman with a blonde bun.
- Eyes: ordinary. The bald man has puffy, bruised-looking eyes.
- Outfit: the bald man wears a white pinstripe suit, dark vest and purple-striped tie. The women wear a cream striped turtleneck and a pink jacket.
- Accessories: none.
- Other forms: none. They appear in the gold-walled temple hall where Geto sits on a dais.
- Tell apart from: Geto's "family" (Miguel, Larue, the twins, Manami, Negi). The believers are ordinary-looking civilians.
